Qolha (قلها) — Anonymous Expression Platform

“Say what you cannot say” — A unified, production-ready PERN web application blending anonymous Q&A, emotional venting, community opinion court, interactive polls, active session management, and provider-agnostic payments into a sleek Arabic-first glassmorphic UI.

✨ Core Platform Features

💬 Anonymous Q&A Engine

Share a unique personal link, receive secret questions from friends or followers, and choose which ones to answer publicly on your profile with engagement metrics.

💭 Vent (بُح) & Mood Express

Express feelings and thoughts anonymously tagged by mood and category. Fellow users react with empathy emojis to provide support without invasive comments.

⚖️ Court of Opinion (المحكمة المجتمعية)

File a public case between plaintiff and defendant. The community votes on who is right, featuring VIP boosted cases and community deliberation badges.

📊 Interactive Polls & Points System

Pro and Star members create interactive polls. Users earn engagement points for participating, climbing daily and weekly community leaderboards.

💻 Active Device Sessions & Revocation

Users can inspect all active logged-in device sessions (IP, User Agent, Location, Last Active) and revoke suspicious sessions instantly with one click.

💳 Provider-Agnostic Payment Layer

Three-tier membership (Free / Pro / Star) powered by a non-Stripe, provider-agnostic service layer ready to connect to Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, or PayPal.

🛡️ Layered Role Authority System

Strict role hierarchy preventing unauthorized demotions or administrative bypasses:

Role Permissions & Authority Scope
superadmin ★ Highest system authority. The ONLY role that can assign or change user roles. Assigned manually in DB.
admin Manages users (bans/deletions/plans), billing revenue, and content moderation. Cannot alter roles or target superadmins.
moderator Content moderation ONLY (hiding/flagging/deleting vents, cases, polls, and answers). Zero access to user data or revenue.
user Regular member with access to Q&A, venting, voting, and upgrading to Pro/Star plans.
